About

Coordinated legal representation across jurisdictions, under one roof

Who we are

KRA Legal Overseas LLP works with businesses and individuals whose legal needs don't sit neatly inside one country. Our practice is built around the India–UAE corridor: helping Indian companies expand into the Gulf, and helping UAE-based and international clients operate confidently in India.

Rather than handing a matter between disconnected local counsel, we keep it under one coordinated team — so advice stays consistent, timelines stay predictable, and someone is always accountable for the outcome.

Cross-border by design

Matters touching India, the UAE, or both are handled by the same team start to finish.

Sector-aware advice

Guidance shaped by how business is actually done in each market.

Direct access to partners

Clients work directly with senior lawyers, not layers of hand-offs.

What we value

Our values

Our values are at the heart of how KRA approaches every matter. We combine integrity, precision and accountability with a practical understanding of our clients' needs. From the first consultation to the resolution of a matter, we remain focused on clear communication, thoughtful legal strategy and dependable execution — building relationships based on trust and delivering counsel with purpose.

Integrity

Straight answers, even when they're not the ones a client hoped for.

Precision

Careful drafting and advice that holds up under scrutiny.

Accessibility

Responsive communication, no unnecessary jargon.

Accountability

One team stays responsible for a matter from open to close.

Our story

How we got here

KRA Legal Overseas LLP has grown with a clear purpose — to make legal support more connected, accessible and effective for clients whose interests extend across borders. From our beginnings in India to our presence in the UAE, every step has been shaped by the needs of the businesses, investors and individuals we serve.

[2001]

Firm founded

KRA began its journey with a focused legal practice built around trusted client relationships and practical legal advice. The foundation was laid with a commitment to integrity, precision and long-term representation.

[2025]

UAE Office opens

The firm expanded its presence into the UAE, strengthening its ability to support clients with interests between India and the Gulf. This marked an important step towards building a truly cross-jurisdictional legal practice.

Today

Continued growth

KRA continues to grow as a cross-border legal practice, supporting businesses, investors and individuals across India and the UAE. Our work spans corporate, disputes, regulatory and private client matters, with a continued focus on practical and commercially meaningful legal solutions.
